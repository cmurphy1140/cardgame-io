HOW TO PLAY · 3 OF 5
Trump
{{ ch.label }}

After the bid, the winner names a trump suit. Every card of that suit outranks every card of the other three, whatever its face.

You must follow the suit that was led if you can. If you cannot, you may play a trump to take the trick, or throw off any card.

The five of trump is the card to catch. It scores five points to whichever team wins the trick it falls in, and that is where the game gets its name.

ABOUT
How Catch 5 is built

Catch 5 is a trick-taking game for four, played in pairs. This app was written for one family, and it keeps to that: no accounts, no adverts, nothing to buy.

The deck

A standard 52-card deck, dealt nine to a seat. Ranks and suits are set in New York and drawn as text, so they scale with your type size and never blur.

The opponents

Otto, Hazel and Rue play from the same rules you do. They see only their own hands and the cards that have been played. Standard opponents count trumps and remember what has fallen; beginner opponents do not.

Drawn, not painted

Nothing in the app is an image. The felt, the box, the cards and the frames around the portraits are drawn in code from a fixed seed, so they are sharp at any size and identical every time you open it.

Your data

Saved matches live on this phone. Nothing leaves it.

The densest text. H1 New York 36/42 bold · H2 New York 26/32 bold, 6 pt extra above · body SF 19/27, paragraph gap 20. All ivory on bare board, {{ crBoard }}. Column 24 pt margins, max 480 pt centred on wider devices. No plates, no rules between sections: rhythm comes from the serif headings alone. Texture under text stays ≤ .07 α so no fleck ever pulls a letter below 10:1.
