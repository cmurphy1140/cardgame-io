Read START-HERE.md, LOOP.md and DESIGN-SYSTEM.md in catch5_design_review/, then look at every
PNG in catch5_design_review/screens/. Open the .dc.html files in design/ if you need exact
values.

This is round 1 of 3. The loop ends when MainMenuView (screen 1a) runs on a phone at Large,
XXXL and AX2 and is approved — sequence your thinking around that, and keep anything not
needed for 1a out of the review (one line in docs/ideas.md instead).

Do not write any UI code this round. Your job is to be a sounding board: read the repo
(Theme.swift, RootView.swift, Cast.swift, Settings.swift, MatchHistory.swift,
docs/screen-flow.md), then write design-review.md in the six-section format START-HERE.md
gives — read-back, feasibility per screen, where the Swift shape should change the design,
cheap wins we are missing, the sequencing you would follow, and your questions and
disagreements.

There is no north star here: the paper direction is consensus, not law, and Apple's HIG and
the research behind these screens are guidelines rather than rules. Say where the design is
swimming upstream of the platform or the data model. Then stop.
