QUESTIONS I COULD NOT ANSWER FROM THE REPO

  1. Box or paper as the frame. E.8 (agreed Sept 14) makes the deck box the frame and the main menu the closed box. Paper replaces the menu. Either paper sheets sit inside the burgundy well (1i option A) or paper is the frame and the box survives only as the table's header band (option B). This decides RootView's base and whether Catch 5 Box 2b still ships.
  2. Does the table's header band go paper? You said the felt is untouched; the band was box. If timers live on it (1d) it stays burgundy in these wireframes.
  3. HARD strength does not exist. The engine has Easy (frozen) and Standard; D22 parked stronger play. Murphy's Law can be a preset that sets Standard bots, Quick speed, beginner mode off and hints off, or a new player. Which is it, and what does Beginner's Luck do beyond Easy + beginner mode? A scoring handicap (bots start at −5) is cheap and honest about being a handicap.
  4. Roster vs the fixed cast. Cast.swift hard-codes Hazel, Otto and Rue at seats 1–3 and Settings stores seatNames. Retiring them means a migration for saved settings and history that name them, and the tutorial text that says "Rue is dealing" (R27).
  5. Timers and beginner mode. Random legal action on timeout will teach a beginner nothing. Proposal: timers are only offered in Pass and Play; beginner mode forces OFF. Confirm.
  6. Which seat does the bot fill with three humans? Wireframe assumes the seat left of the host (West) so the host's partner is always human. Alternative: the bot partners the host so the two guests play as a team.
  7. Joined date. Settings has no creation date. For existing installs use the earliest MatchRecord.date, else today. Confirm that a licence can say "joined" before the first match.
  8. Standing thresholds are placeholders (Rookie / Regular / Shark). Also: does standing count pass-and-play wins? Wallet does not; standing probably should not either, or a friend can farm it.
  9. Chips for Murphy's Law. Same 50 for a win at every strength invites playing Beginner's Luck for chips. A multiplier (Easy ×0.5, Standard ×1, Murphy ×2) or flat pay: your call.
  10. Pit boss without a server. Filing a GitHub issue from the app needs a token or a login. AGENTS.md rules out hosting and paid services. A prefilled github.com/cmurphy1140/catch-5/issues/new?title=…&body=… URL opened in Safari needs nothing and puts the last hand in the body as text. Acceptable?
  11. Who sees the board. You said both. The app cannot read docs/roadmap.md at runtime; a build script can export a JSON of the three columns into the bundle the way RulesText mirrors the rules doc, with a test that they match.
  12. Fonts. Barlow is OFL and can be bundled (two families, six files, Info.plist entries). Or SF with .fontWidth(.condensed) and .fontDesign(.default): no bundling, Dynamic Type for free, slightly less character. The licence's 9 pt labels are below the 12 pt floor; on the phone they need to be 11 pt minimum and scale with the boost (Theme.textBoostSteps = 2 already makes Large read as XXL).

IDEAS TO TUNE

  • Licence renewal. A licence expires visually (a faint EXPIRED stamp) after 30 days without a match and un-expires on the next one. No penalty; a nudge in the object's own language.
  • Endorsement for beating a Shark. The roster is the achievement list's best friend: one badge per bot beaten as a partner-less opponent (7 badges), which also gives the dossier's "your record" a reason to exist.
  • Dealer's button. A small ink chip with D on the dealer's seat tile, since the deal passes left and the rules make the dealer special (must bid 2, may match). Felt change, so it waits, but it is one glyph.
  • Season = month. Statistics' THIS SEASON is the calendar month; the licence shows the month's wins as a small secondary number. No server needed.
  • House advice lines can come from the loser: after a lost match the next launch's tip is the winning bot's quip. Same array, one extra rule.
  • Re-deal has a rules cost. Real tables re-deal only on a misdeal. If it is sold, it should be before bidding only and once per match, and the hand record should say so (review honesty, D24).
  • Undo already exists free (M4). Charging chips for it in bot play changes a shipped decision. Suggest: Undo stays free in beginner mode, costs 10 outside it; or drop it from the shop.
  • Card backs and felt colours are the only shop items that touch the felt. They must keep the green back's contrast with ivory faces and the five gold meanings (D33): burgundy felt needs a gold check, midnight is fine.
  • Pass and play as the family screen. Two humans as partners is the E.6 idea in disguise: between hands they can talk strategy legally. The handoff card could show a "partner's note" text field that the partner sees on their next handoff: one line, public to the two of them, illegal at a real table only during a hand.
